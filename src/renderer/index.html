How the expiration is calculated

The expiration field projects how long the tooling can keep running based on production data.

  • Remaining (Qty) = Tooling Life − Produced
  • Days Available = (Remaining (Qty) ÷ Annual Volume) × 365
  • If Annual Volume is 0 or empty, we use: Remaining (Qty) × 365 days.
  • We add the calculated days to the Production Date.
Expiration = Production Date + ( Remaining (Qty) Annual Volume × 365 days )
Example: if Remaining (Qty) = 4,000, Annual Volume = 2,000, and Production Date = 01/01/2024, we have (4000 ÷ 2000) × 365 = 730 days. Adding 730 days to 01/01/2024 gives Expiration = 01/01/2026.

When to update the Production Date

Every time you change the Produced quantity, record the production date used for that entry. This keeps the lifecycle timeline accurate.

  • The date should reflect when the new Produced total became valid.
  • Reminders highlight the field after Produced changes to catch your attention.
  • Without the date, expiration projections and reports can become misleading.
Tip: update both fields together—first Produced, then Production Date—so reminders disappear automatically.

About Analysis Completed

This field is hidden by default and only appears when the tooling is expired.

  • Use it after reviewing the expired tooling and confirming that no further revitalization action is required.
  • When the checkbox is selected, the tooling will no longer be listed as expired in the interface metrics and lists.
  • You can uncheck it at any time if the tooling needs to appear again in the expired follow-up lists.
